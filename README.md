# Helix Hustle (CHEM 3B Chapter 12: Nucleic Acids and Protein Synthesis)

Study game for CHEM 3B nucleic acids and protein synthesis. Planned home: https://itsdrdan.github.io/nucleicacids/

- `index.html` is the whole game (no build step). It loads the brand kit (`games/brand/itsdrdan.css`, `icons.js`, `embed.js`), `games/track.js` for anonymous stats (game id `nucleicacids`), and the Final Boss nucleic acid questions from `textbook/bank/nucleic.json` for Final-exam review.
- Every structure and diagram is drawn by the game's code: the five bases, ribose and deoxyribose (Haworth), nucleosides and nucleotides (including ATP), strands with 5′/3′ ends, base-paired DNA with hydrogen bonds, a replication fork, a tRNA cloverleaf with its codon, a ribosome with P and A sites, and pre-mRNA with introns and exons.
- Content follows Lecture 26 (Timberlake Ch 21) and the Exam 4 study guide learning goals.
- `ASSETS.md` lists the ChatGPT art (logo, 16 badges, 5 moods of Ribo the nucleotide mascot) and where each file goes. Missing art falls back to brand-kit badges, `logo.svg` and a code-drawn mascot.
- Links: `?topic=<id>` opens one topic (e.g. `?topic=transcribe`, `?topic=codons`, `?topic=mutations`); `?mode=blocks|structure|copy|protein|final` opens a whole option. `?course=3B` is accepted and ignored.
- `meta.json` is the study hub card.
